NOT TAX-DEDUCTIBLE · INDEPENDENT OF THE IDF · NO WEAPONS, EVER

Where your money goes

Donations pool in a single fund, held in a dedicated bank account, and are spent on the next verified need. There are no earmarks — pooling is what lets the fund buy without raising for each item first.

How the fund reports

Receipts and records are kept for every purchase. We publish our costs and share what we can about where the money went — without unit names or locations, and not in every case.

What the fund costs to run

There is no staff and no office, and nobody is paid to fundraise. The card-processing fee is the only cost taken out of a gift; the rest is spent on equipment.
