01
The gap

Built for divisions.
Blind to workshops.

Military procurement is built for scale: thousands of rifles, tons of rations, fleets of vehicles. It moves slowly on purpose, because at that scale mistakes are expensive. But a ₪4,000 printer for a unit workshop goes through the same signatures and the same budget committees, and arrives months after it mattered.

The units this fund works with solve technical problems for the ground forces — prototyping fixtures, repairing field equipment, adapting commercial gear to military use. Ask what slows the work down and the answer is rarely skill or motivation. It is a specific item, available in any store, sitting in a queue.

That gap is small enough for private donors to close. This fund exists to close it.

02
The model

How your gift moves

Four stops between your card and a unit workshop. No committees, no warehouses, no middlemen taking a cut.

Stop 01 · Pool

Your donation joins one fund

No campaigns and no earmarks. Pooling is what makes the speed possible: when a need is verified, the money is already there.

Stop 02 · Verify

A need is flagged from inside

Requests come from people serving in the units the fund grew out of, and each one is confirmed with unit leadership before a shekel moves.

Stop 03 · Buy

We purchase retail and deliver

Off the shelf, usually within the week, handed over to the unit through its own leadership.

Stop 04 · Publish

The purchase goes in the ledger

Item, cost, and what it unblocked — posted on this site after delivery, with the receipt on file. Unit names and locations stay off the internet.

Why the fund exists
04
Before you give

Straight answers

A defense-adjacent fund asking for money owes you clarity up front. Here is ours.

Tax status

Donations are not tax-deductible

There is no charity registration behind this fund. US and UK charitable law was not written for foreign military equipment, and we would rather stay fast and say so than stretch the truth. Treat a donation as a personal gift.

Affiliation

Independent of the IDF

The fund is not affiliated with or endorsed by the army. It works with the units, not around them: deliveries are checked with and accepted by unit leadership, but no one in uniform runs this.

Security

The vagueness is deliberate

Unit names, locations, faces, and open equipment gaps stay off this site. Publishing what specific units lack would be a gift to the wrong audience, so purchases are described in general terms, after delivery.

Scope

No weapons of any kind

The fund does not buy weapons, ammunition, weapon accessories, or licensed equipment. Printers, parts, tools, batteries. The line does not move.

05
Who runs it

A named person,
not a black box

The fund is run by Aryeh Lev Mason, working directly with personnel in innovation-focused IDF units. There is no staff and no office; payment-processing fees are the only cost taken out of donations.

Questions before giving are welcome, and answers come from the person who runs the fund: contact@defenseofinnovation.com.
