Where it goes

Into one pool, out to the next need

No earmarks. Pooled money is what makes a same-week purchase possible: when a request is verified, the fund doesn't wait to raise for it.

What you'll see

Every purchase, published

Each purchase appears in the ledger after delivery — the item, the cost, and what it unblocked, without unit names or locations. Receipts stay on file.

What it costs

Overhead is processing fees

No staff, no office, no paid fundraising. Card-processing fees are the only cost taken out of a gift; the rest is spent on equipment.

Larger gifts

Talk to us first

Giving from a foundation or business, or considering a larger amount? Write to contact@defenseofinnovation.com and we'll talk it through before any money moves.
