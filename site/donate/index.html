Fund the next purchase

One pooled fund, no earmarks. Your money buys the next verified need, usually within the week.

Pick an amount

For scale: $180 covers a serious cordless drill, $540 a batch of spare parts, $1,800 a 3D printer with a year of filament.

Smaller gifts carry real weight here too — half of what the fund buys costs less than ₪1,000.

Giving from a foundation or business, or thinking about a larger amount? Write to contact@defenseofinnovation.com and we'll talk it through before any money moves.

NOT TAX-DEDUCTIBLE · INDEPENDENT OF THE IDF · NO WEAPONS, EVER

Where your money goes

Into one pool, out to the next verified need. There are no earmarks — pooled money is what makes a same-week purchase possible, because the fund never has to raise for a specific item before buying it.

How the fund reports

Receipts and records are kept for every purchase, and we share what security allows — the kinds of equipment bought and what they cost, without unit names or locations.

What the fund costs to run

There is no staff and no office, and nobody is paid to fundraise. The card-processing fee is the only cost taken out of a gift; the rest is spent on equipment.
